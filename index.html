FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Will it damage my hair?

I check your hair first, explain the risks honestly and choose the approach that suits it. Your own hair comes first.

Is it suitable for my texture?

Most often, yes. I adapt the technique, for example stretching coily hair for a sleek ponytail, or adjusting the leave-out for fine hair.

How long does it last?

A sew-in lasts 4 to 8 weeks. A ponytail about 1.5 to 2 weeks, half-up half-down about 2 weeks.

How long is the appointment?

About 4 to 5 hours. Plan your day around it.

Do you wash my hair?

No. Please come with washed and blow-dried hair, so we can start right away.

What if something comes loose?

Message me. If it's down to my work, I look at it and fix it. Best within the first 1 to 2 weeks.
